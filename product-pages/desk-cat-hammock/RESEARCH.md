# The Overhang: research notes

Product: Adjustable Desk Cat Hammock with clamp and feather toy (AliExpress item 1005012938918328, cost £20.09).

## What the page states, and where it comes from

| Claim on the page | Source |
|---|---|
| Ring Ø 40 cm, wire 0.8 cm, head plate 8 × 3 cm at 0.4 cm, pin Ø 1.0 × 4.5 cm | Supplier's factory drawing (`photos/factory-drawing.webp`) |
| Grey mesh cover with gathered elastic edge and a stitched opening | Supplier photo (`photos/mesh-cover-cutout.webp`) |
| Clamp, feather toy included | Listing title |
| Clamp opens to 5 cm | Most common figure across clones of this design (1–5 cm). **Not confirmed for this listing. Check it.** Change `clampMaxMm` in the script and the spec table if it differs. |
| Big breeds may find a 40 cm ring tight | Owner reviews of desk hammocks (Maine Coons couldn't turn around) |
| Some cats need days, treats or catnip before they use it | Owner reviews on Chewy and Walmart listings |
| Clamp, not adhesive | Owner review where 3M tape failed after about 10 days |
| Unhook the feather toy when unattended | General vet guidance on string ingestion |

## Left out on purpose

- "Desk Nest" is a registered brand for this kind of product. The name is not used anywhere.
- No weight rating. The listing's figure couldn't be read, and clones claim anywhere from 35 to 50 lb.
- No reviews, star ratings, customer counts or press logos. None exist for this product yet.
- No claim about height adjustment. "Adjustable" in the title may only mean the clamp.

## Placeholders to set before going live

- Price: `$49.00` in the HTML and `PRODUCT.price` in the script.
- Checkout: the cart drawer is front-end only until it's wired to the store's product.

## Photos

AliExpress, alicdn, eBay, Amazon, Unsplash and Pexels were all blocked by this environment's network policy. Only the two photos supplied with the brief were used. Everything else on the page is a 3D model or illustration built from the factory drawing.
