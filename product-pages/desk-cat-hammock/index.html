Moving in

Some cats move in within the hour. Some take a week.

Owners of desk hammocks report both. These four steps are how to tip the odds.

  1. 1

    Clamp it next to your chair, on the side your cat usually comes from.

  2. 2

    Put in something that smells like you. A T-shirt you've worn works well.

  3. 3

    Drop a treat on the mesh, then ignore them. Cats prefer ideas they think were theirs.

  4. 4

    Leave it up for a week before you judge it. Plenty of cats spend a few days being suspicious first.

Questions

Before you clamp it on.

How big a cat does it suit?

The ring is 40 cm across, which suits most adult house cats curled up. Very large breeds may find it snug. Use the to-scale view above with your cat's measurement.

Will the clamp mark my desk?

The jaws press on the surface they grip. On soft veneer or a painted top, put a folded cloth or a felt pad under each jaw before you tighten it.

Does it really swivel?

Yes. The ring turns on a 1 cm steel pin that sits in the clamp's sleeve. Swing it alongside the desk when you need room, and back out when your cat wants the view.

Can I use it on a glass desk?

No. A clamp puts a lot of pressure on a small area, and glass doesn't take that well.

How do I wash the cover?

Unhook the elastic edge from the ring, hand-wash the cover in cool water and hang it to dry. Wipe the ring and clamp with a damp cloth.

Is it OK for kittens?

Yes, while you're nearby. Take the feather toy off when you're not, and keep an eye on very small kittens until they're confident getting in and out.

What if my cat ignores it?

Give it a week. Move it closer to your chair, add something that smells like you, and leave a treat on the mesh. Some cats are in within the hour; others need a few days of suspicion first.
