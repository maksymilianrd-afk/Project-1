Small print

The small print, in big letters.

Smells like cake. Isn't cake.

The flavour is a scent. Don't eat it, and keep it where small children can't get to it.

Wipe the tip.

Whatever touches the tip goes back on your lips. A quick tissue swipe after use keeps it clean. Don't share it if you have a cold sore.

Not for the dashboard.

Lip butter softens in heat. Leave it in a hot car and it goes runny, and runny balm can leak past the cap.

A little goes a long way.

Like most glossy balms, it can feel a bit sticky when you first put it on. A pea-size dot is plenty. More just means more stick.

Patch test if you're sensitive.

Scent ingredients are a common reason lips react. If yours sting or swell, wipe it off and stop using it.

No SPF.

It doesn't protect against sun. On bright days, wear an SPF lip balm.
