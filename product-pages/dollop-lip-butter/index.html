Good to know

Is it tinted?

Lightly. Each shade gives a sheer wash of colour, so your natural lip colour shows through. Vanilla Pod is almost clear.

Does it feel sticky?

Glossy balms can feel slightly tacky at first. A pea-sized amount covers both lips and keeps it light.

Does it contain SPF?

No. It doesn't protect against the sun, so wear an SPF lip product outdoors.

I have sensitive lips. Can I use it?

Check the ingredients on the box and patch test on your inner wrist first. Scent ingredients are a common cause of irritation. Stop using it if your lips sting, itch or swell.

Is it edible?

No. The shades are scented, not flavoured. Keep it away from young children.

What happens if it gets warm?

It softens with warmth. Keep it out of direct sun and hot cars, where it can turn runny.

How do I keep it clean?

Wipe the tip after use and replace the cap. Don't share it.
