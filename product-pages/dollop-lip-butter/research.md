## Decisions this changed on the page

- **Name.** The tubes in the photos carry another company's trademark, so the page uses an original brand, **Dollop**, and an original 3D tube. None of the listing photos appear on the page. Shade names are new as well: Spun Sugar, Vanilla Pod, Cold Brew, Cake Day.
- **No formula claims.** The copy doesn't claim shea, hyaluronic acid, vegan, cruelty-free, SPF or "non-toxic", because none of that can be verified for this supplier. The copy sticks to what the listing and photos show: 15 g squeeze tube, twist-off cap, sheer tint, glossy finish, dessert scents.
- **Honest small print.** The sticker section tells people it can feel sticky, softens in heat, needs a wiped tip, isn't food, has no SPF, and should be patch-tested. These are the things reviewers complain about and the real risks of a scented lip product.
- **No reviews.** The page has no reviews section. Add one only when there are real customer reviews.

## Before this goes live

1. Source an unbranded or own-label version of the tube. Selling tubes printed with the Summer Fridays mark is selling counterfeits (in the UK, an offence under s.92 of the Trade Marks Act 1994), whatever the page says.
2. UK cosmetics rules apply: a UK Responsible Person, a Cosmetic Product Safety Report for each shade, and notification on the SCPN portal before sale.
3. Paste the supplier's full INCI list into "What's in it" (there is a TODO comment in `index.html`).
4. Check prices, delivery threshold and returns policy. They live in `CONFIG` at the top of the main script and in the "Before you buy" section: £12 each, any three for £30, free UK delivery over £25, unopened returns within 30 days.
5. Check each shade's tint against a real tube and edit the `FLAVOURS` list if needed.
