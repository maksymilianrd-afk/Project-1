The build

From the sand up.

Nine stages, in the order you build them. Keep scrolling and he goes together brick by brick.

  1. 01

    Desert base

    Tan plates and loose rubble on a black plinth. Both boots lock into it, which is why he never tips.

  2. 02

    First leg

    Boot, grey undersuit and a yellow knee plate, built up from the sole.

  3. 03

    Second leg

    The mirror of the first, with the knee darts on the outside and a pouch strapped to the thigh.

  4. 04

    Hips and belt

    The hips close the lower half. A brown belt goes on with its row of pouches.

  5. 05

    Torso

    The grey core of the suit, with exposed studs down the sides and a dark green plate over the stomach.

  6. 06

    Arms

    Shoulder joints, grey sleeves, dark red gauntlets and two five-finger hands.

  7. 07

    Helmet

    Cheeks, the black T-visor in its dark red frame, a layered dome and the rangefinder on its clip.

  8. 08

    Jetpack, cape, chest plates

    Blue tanks with orange bands, a rocket riding on top, the green chest armour and the fabric cape.

  9. 09

    Carbine and plaque

    The last stage. He gets his carbine, the mini figure gets its stand and you get the plaque.

    Before you start

    Six things to know before you build.

    SORT

    Sort before you build.

    1,544 pieces is a lot of grey. Trays by colour save a lot of hunting later, in the legs especially.

    COUNT

    Check each stage's parts first.

    Third-party kits can come up a piece short. Count against the parts list before each stage so you find out before the helmet, not halfway through it.

    VISOR

    Take your time on the face.

    The dark red frame around the visor sets the whole look. Line each piece up to the bottom and outer edges before you press it down.

    CAPE

    Let the creases drop.

    The cape arrives folded. Hang it for a day, or steam it from a distance, and the fold lines relax.

    SUN

    Keep him out of direct sun.

    UV fades and yellows plastic over time, light colours most. A shelf away from the window keeps the armour green.

    LIFT

    Carry him by the base.

    The rangefinder and the rocket are clipped on and are the first parts to come off. Lift from the plinth, never the helmet.
