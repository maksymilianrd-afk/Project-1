01 · What it is

It’s not gas neon.That’s the good news.

Glass neon is a tube of gas and a transformer that hums. This is a strip of LEDs sealed in an 8 mm silicone sleeve, running on 5 volts from a USB port. No hum, no glass, and it gets warm rather than hot.

LED doesn’t stutter when it comes on, either. The flicker at the top of the page was us showing off.

03 · Frame colour

Red or amber.Same night.

The edge light tints everything near it. Red pushes the print towards hot pink; amber warms it towards a late sunset. Watch the page while you switch. It takes the colour too.

04 · The build

Four layers,35 mm deep.

  1. 01Clear backAcrylic, with two hanging holes drilled along the top edge.
  2. 02Printed panelThe night beach, printed flat. The depth is all in the drawing.
  3. 03Lit frameLED flex around the whole edge, in a 25 mm ABS frame.
  4. 04R + starWhite channels outlined in Ø8 mm silicone LED flex. Power comes in through a 5.5 × 2.1 mm DC socket.

05 · Mounting

Wall,or a chain.

It’s listed as double-sided, so it doesn’t have to sit flat on a wall. Screw it up, hang it on the hook, or let it swing on the chain from a ceiling hook, a shelf or a window frame.

Screws, hook and chain are in the box. Planning to hang it in a window? Ask us for a photo of the back first.

08 · Before you buy

Read thisfirst.

  1. UnofficialFan-made decor. It isn’t made, licensed or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
  2. No plug in the boxThe lead ends in USB-A. A 5 V phone charger rated 1.2 A or more will run it, and so will most laptops and power banks.
  3. Indoors onlyKeep it dry and out of direct sun. Sunlight fades printed colour and yellows plastic over time.
  4. Hands off the neonThe flex is fixed to the panel. Don’t bend the panel, and don’t lift the sign by its cable.
  5. Supplier figuresSize, weight and power come from the supplier’s spec sheet. If a unit measures differently, we’ll fix this page.
  6. Neon photographs badlyEvery camera gets it slightly wrong, ours included. Colours on your screen won’t match the room exactly.
